# Robustness & Distribution Shift Papers

This index reviews methods to ensure neural networks remain reliable under out-of-distribution shifts, corruptions, and intentional perturbations.

---

## Featured Papers

### 1. Towards Deep Learning Models Resistant to Adversarial Attacks (2017)
* **Authors:** Aleksander Madry, Aleksandar Makelov, Ludwig Schmidt, Dimitris Tsipras, Adrian Vladu
* **Focus:** Projected Gradient Descent (PGD), adversarial training, min-max optimization
* **Description:** Formulates adversarial robustness as a robust optimization problem and establishes PGD-based adversarial training as a standard defense benchmark.
* **Link:** [arXiv:1706.06083](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06083)

### 2. Benchmarking Neural Network Robustness to Common Corruptions and Perturbations (ImageNet-C) (2019)
* **Authors:** Dan Hendrycks, Thomas Dietterich
* **Focus:** Out-of-distribution generalization, natural corruptions, robustness metrics
* **Description:** Introduces standard benchmark datasets (ImageNet-C, ImageNet-P) to systematically test model performance under realistic domain shifts and blur/noise corruptions.
* **Link:** [arXiv:1903.12261](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.12261)

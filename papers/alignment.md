# Alignment Research Papers

This section tracks papers focusing on aligning agent goals with human intent, RLHF/RLAIF mechanisms, debate, scalable oversight, and inner alignment.

---

## Featured Papers

### 1. Deep Reinforcement Learning from Human Preferences (2017)
* **Authors:** Paul Christiano, Jan Leike, Tom B. Brown, Miljan Martic, Shane Legg, Dario Amodei
* **Focus:** RLHF, human feedback, reward learning
* **Description:** Introduces the core algorithmic framework for using human pairwise preferences to train reward models for complex RL agents.
* **Link:** [arXiv:1706.03741](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741)

### 2. Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback (InstructGPT) (2022)
* **Authors:** Long Ouyang, Jeff Wu, Xu Jiang, Diogo Almeida, Carroll Wainwright, et al.
* **Focus:** Instruction tuning, RLHF, alignment fine-tuning
* **Description:** Demonstrates that applying RLHF to large language models significantly improves helpfulness, honesty, and safety compliance over raw base models.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2203.02155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155)

### 3. Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback (2022)
* **Authors:** Yuntao Bai, Saurav Kadavath, Sandipan Kundu, Amanda Askell, et al. (Anthropic)
* **Focus:** RLAIF, automated alignment, principle-based training
* **Description:** Proposes training aligned AI models using explicit constitutional principles and self-critique, replacing manual human annotations.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073)

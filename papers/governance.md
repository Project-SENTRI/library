# AI Governance Research Papers

This index indexes research on compute governance, regulatory frameworks, verification mechanisms, and international policies.

---

## Featured Papers

### 1. Computing Power and the Governance of Artificial Intelligence (2024)
* **Authors:** Lennart Heim, Markus Anderljung, Joslyn Barnhart, et al.
* **Focus:** Compute tracking, hardware governance, chip controls
* **Description:** Analyzes specialized compute hardware as a critical control point for tracking, auditing, and regulating frontier AI capabilities.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2402.08797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08797)

### 2. Frontiers of AI Governance: Proposals for Managing Advanced AI Models (2023)
* **Authors:** Markus Anderljung, Joscha Bach, Toby Ord, Alan Dafoe, et al.
* **Focus:** Licensing, audit standards, red teaming, deployment pauses
* **Description:** Proposes actionable regulatory tools including pre-deployment auditing, capability evaluations, and licensing standards for developers of highly capable frontier models.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2307.03718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03718)

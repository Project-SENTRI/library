# Evaluation & Capability Testing Papers

This section indexed research methodologies for evaluating capabilities, autonomy, refusal boundaries, and dangerous skills in AI models.

---

## Featured Papers

### 1. Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM) (2022)
* **Authors:** Percy Liang, Rishi Bommasani, Tony Lee, et al. (Stanford CRFM)
* **Focus:** Multi-metric evaluation, standardized benchmarks, model taxonomy
* **Description:** Establishes a comprehensive framework for evaluating models across accuracy, calibration, robustness, bias, toxicity, and efficiency simultaneously.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2211.09110](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110)

### 2. Evaluating Emergent Abilities and Autonomous Capabilities in Frontier AI (2023)
* **Authors:** METR / ARC Evals Team
* **Focus:** Autonomous agent evaluation, replication capability, cyber risk testing
* **Description:** Outlines methodologies for testing whether frontier LLM agents possess dangerous autonomous capabilities, such as self-replication or automated cyber operations.
* **Link:** [METR Research Publications](https://metr.org/research/)

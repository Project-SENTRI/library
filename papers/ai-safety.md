# AI Safety Research Papers

This index highlights foundational and modern papers addressing systemic risks, catastrophic hazards, power-seeking behavior, and safe deployment frameworks.

---

## Featured Papers

### 1. Concrete Problems in AI Safety (2016)
* **Authors:** Dario Amodei, Chris Olah, Jacob Steinhardt, Paul Christiano, John Schulman, Dan Mane
* **Focus:** Reward hacking, safe exploration, scalable oversight, side effects
* **Description:** A seminal paper outlining practical, technical research problems in preventing unintended machine learning behavior.
* **Link:** [arXiv:1606.06565](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565)

### 2. Unsolved Problems in ML Safety (2021)
* **Authors:** Dan Hendrycks, Nicholas Carlini, John Schulman, Thomas Steinhardt
* **Focus:** Robustness, monitoring, alignment, systemic safety
* **Description:** Outlines key technical bottlenecks in machine learning safety to guide research priorities toward practical risk reduction.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2109.13916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13916)

### 3. Managing AI Risks in an Era of Rapid Progress (2023)
* **Authors:** Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton, Andrew Yao, Dawn Song, et al.
* **Focus:** Catastrophic risks, frontier deployment, regulatory safeguards
* **Description:** A consensus paper by prominent AI scientists calling for technical safeguards and governance mechanisms for advanced AI models.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2310.18613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18613)

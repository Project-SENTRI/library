# AI Risk Management Frameworks

A curated directory of methodologies, impact assessment tools, and risk evaluation frameworks designed to identify, measure, and mitigate hazards across the AI lifecycle.

> **Note** > Project SENTRI does not own or publish these frameworks. This directory exists solely to help learners, researchers, and practitioners navigate official risk management resources.

## Core Lifecycle Functions (e.g., NIST AI RMF)

- **Govern:** Instill a culture of risk management and clear policies.
- **Map:** Contextualize and categorize potential risks specific to the domain.
- **Measure:** Analyze, assess, and benchmark risks using quantitative and qualitative metrics.
- **Manage:** Allocate resources to respond to, mitigate, and monitor identified risks continuously.

## Key Governance Dimensions

When evaluating AI Governance frameworks, consider these four core pillars:
1. **Accountability:** Defining who owns the risk for AI outcomes and system behavior.
2. **Transparency & Auditability:** Maintaining clear records of training data, model decisions, and human oversight.
3. **Regulatory Compliance:** Adhering to legal standards across relevant jurisdictions (e.g., EU AI Act, FTC guidelines).
4. **Human Oversight:** Designing human-in-the-loop mechanisms for high-stakes decision-making.

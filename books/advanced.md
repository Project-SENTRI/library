# Advanced Technical AI Safety & ML Books

These texts cover deep mathematical principles, adversarial robustness, reinforcement learning, formal methods, and modern machine learning theory.

---

## Featured Books

### 1. Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach (4th Edition)
* **Authors:** Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig
* **Topics:** Classical AI, Probabilistic Reasoning, Reinforcement Learning, Safety & Ethics
* **Description:** The standard university-level textbook covering foundational AI algorithms, state-space search, probabilistic decision-making, and modern machine learning principles.
* **Link:** [Pearson Publisher Page](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/artificial-intelligence-a-modern-approach/P200000003500)

### 2. Deep Learning
* **Authors:** Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville
* **Topics:** Neural Network Theory, Optimization, Generative Models, Deep Architectures
* **Description:** The definitive mathematical textbook on deep learning, covering linear algebra foundations, numerical optimization, autoencoders, and deep generative architectures.
* **Link:** [Freely Available Online](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)

### 3. Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd Edition)
* **Authors:** Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto
* **Topics:** MDPs, Policy Gradients, Q-Learning, Temporal Difference Learning
* **Description:** The foundational textbook on RL. Understanding RL agent dynamics, reward mechanisms, and policy optimization is critical for modern RLHF and alignment work.
* **Link:** [Freely Available Online (PDF)](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html)

### 4. Convex Optimization
* **Authors:** Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe
* **Topics:** Optimization Theory, Convex Sets, Duality, Mathematical Foundations
* **Description:** Essential theoretical foundation for understanding loss landscapes, machine learning optimization bounds, and formal verification frameworks.
* **Link:** [Freely Available Online (Stanford)](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/)

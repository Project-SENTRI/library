# Intermediate AI Safety & Security Books

These books bridge conceptual AI safety with practical technical implementations, basic mathematical frameworks, algorithmic ethics, and cybersecurity fundamentals.

---

## Featured Books

### 1. The Ethical Algorithm: The Science of Socially Aware Algorithm Design
* **Authors:** Michael Kearns and Aaron Roth
* **Topics:** Algorithmic Fairness, Differential Privacy, Game Theory, AI Ethics
* **Description:** Provides an accessible yet rigorous look at how to design algorithmic constraints directly into machine learning models for privacy, fairness, and safety.
* **Link:** [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-ethical-algorithm-9780190948207)

### 2. Deep Learning with Python (2nd Edition)
* **Author:** François Chollet
* **Topics:** Deep Learning Fundamentals, PyTorch/Keras, Neural Networks
* **Description:** An practical, code-first introduction to deep learning architecture written by the creator of Keras, establishing essential technical knowledge for downstream AI safety work.
* **Link:** [Manning Publications](https://www.manning.com/books/deep-learning-with-python-second-edition)

### 3. Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies
* **Author:** Nick Bostrom
* **Topics:** Existential Risk, Strategic Alignment, Orthogonality Thesis, Instrumental Goals
* **Description:** A seminal academic foundation analyzing the theoretical risks, intelligence explosion dynamics, and strategic levers associated with artificial general intelligence.
* **Link:** [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/superintelligence-9780199678112)

### 4. Cybersecurity and Cyberwar: What Everyone Needs to Know
* **Authors:** P.W. Singer and Allan Friedman
* **Topics:** Cyber Security, Threat Modeling, Defensive Infrastructure
* **Description:** Essential reading for understanding foundational security threat modeling, vulnerability exploit pathways, and defensive frameworks relevant to AI security.
* **Link:** [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/cybersecurity-and-cyberwar-9780199918119)

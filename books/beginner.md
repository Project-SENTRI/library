# Beginner AI Safety & Ethics Books

These foundational books provide accessible, high-level entries into AI safety, alignment, ethics, and machine learning history. No advanced technical or mathematical background is required.

---

## Featured Books

### 1. The Alignment Problem: Machine Learning and Human Values
* **Author:** Brian Christian
* **Topics:** AI Alignment, Value Learning, Reinforcement Learning History, Ethics
* **Description:** A compelling look at how machine learning models learn human biases, goals, and implicit values, detailing the history and challenge of aligning modern AI systems.
* **Link:** [Publisher Page](https://wwnorton.com/books/9780393635829)

### 2. Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control
* **Author:** Stuart Russell
* **Topics:** AI Safety, Provably Beneficial AI, AI Governance, Instrumental Convergence
* **Description:** Written by one of the co-authors of the standard AI textbook (*Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*), this book explains why standard AI design goals lead to control problems and introduces beneficial AI principles.
* **Link:** [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/600683/human-compatible-by-stuart-russell/)

### 3. Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society
* **Author:** Dan Hendrycks (Center for AI Safety)
* **Topics:** AI Safety Fundamentals, Governance, Malicious Use, Systemic Risk, Ethics
* **Description:** A comprehensive open-access introductory textbook designed specifically for students and practitioners entering the field of AI safety.
* **Link:** [Freely Available Online](https://www.aisafetybook.com/)

### 4. Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence
* **Author:** Max Tegmark
* **Topics:** AGI Futures, AI Governance, Existential Risk, Economic Impact
* **Description:** Explores the medium- and long-term consequences of advanced artificial intelligence on human society, economy, and global governance.
* **Link:** [Vintage Books](https://www.maxtegmark.org/life30)

### 5. Atlas of AI: Power, Politics, and the Planetary Costs of Artificial Intelligence
* **Author:** Kate Crawford
* **Topics:** AI Ethics, Resource Consumption, Data Privacy, Sociotechnical Systems
* **Description:** Investigates the physical, environmental, political, and material infrastructure required to power modern AI systems.
* **Link:** [Yale University Press](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300209570/atlas-of-ai/)

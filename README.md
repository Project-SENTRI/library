# Project SENTRI Library

The Project SENTRI Library is a curated knowledge base dedicated to AI safety, AI security, and responsible AI.
Rather than acting as a repository of files, the Library serves as an organized index of trusted resources—including research papers, books, courses, datasets, tools, frameworks, and organizations—to help students, developers, researchers, and practitioners navigate the field
Our goal is to make reliable information easier to discover while encouraging evidence-based learning and responsible AI development.

## Principles

The Library is curated according to the following principles:

- **Accuracy** – Resources should come from reliable and reputable sources.
- **Accessibility** – Whenever possible, freely available resources are prioritized.
- **Relevance** – Content should contribute meaningfully to AI safety, AI security, or responsible AI.
- **Organization** – Resources are categorized to make exploration intuitive.
- **Maintenance** – Outdated or superseded resources are reviewed periodically.

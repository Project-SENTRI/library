# Monthly Newsletters

A curated collection of trusted monthly and bi-weekly digests providing high-level overviews, long-form research breakdowns, and strategic insights across AI safety, governance, cybersecurity, and machine learning.

> **Note** > Project SENTRI does not own, publish, or endorse these newsletters. This directory exists solely to help learners discover reputable educational resources.

## Recommended Reading Order

1. **For Policy & Governance:** Read *GovAI*, *CSET*, and *OECD AI Policy Observatory*.
2. **For Big-Picture AI Safety:** Read *Future of Life Institute* and *Epoch AI*.
3. **For Cybersecurity Leadership:** Read *SANS NewsBites* and *Schneier on Security*.

## Notes

- Monthly newsletters are ideal if you want macro-level analysis without overloading your inbox daily or weekly.
- All archives are free and publicly accessible.

## Recommended Reading Order

1. **For Technical AI Safety:** Start with *The ML Safety Newsletter* (Dan Hendrycks) and *Alignment Forum Digest*.
2. **For Applied ML & Code:** Read *AK's ArXiv Feed*.
3. **For Frontier Lab Research:** Follow *Anthropic Research*, *DeepMind Research*, and *BAIR Blog*.
4. **For AI Security & Red Teaming:** Follow *LLM Security Digest (OWASP)*.

---

## Notes

- Research newsletters frequently link directly to pre-print papers on [arXiv.org](https://arxiv.org).
- Having a basic understanding of linear algebra, calculus, and Python will help when navigating original papers referenced in these digests.

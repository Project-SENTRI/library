## Recommended Self-Study Path

### Baseline Computer Science & ML (Undergraduate Level)
1. **6.S084: Intro to Machine Learning** *(MIT)*
   - *Focus:* Master foundational linear algebra, loss functions, and core ML algorithms via full lectures.
2. **6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning** *(MIT)*
   - *Focus:* Learn modern neural networks, CNNs, and generative models through recorded lectures.

### NLP, Deep Architectures & Reinforcement Learning (Intermediate)
3. **CS 224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning** *(Stanford University)*
   - *Focus:* Deep dive into Transformer architectures, self-attention, and language modeling.
4. **NYU Deep Learning** *(New York University)*
   - *Focus:* Study self-supervised learning, energy-based models, and modern deep architecture theory.
5. **CS 285: Deep Reinforcement Learning** *(UC Berkeley)*
   - *Focus:* Master mathematical RL foundations required to analyze advanced alignment techniques.

### Specialized AI Safety, Alignment & Probing (Graduate / Advanced)
6. **CS 198: Introduction to ML Safety** *(UC Berkeley)*
   - *Focus:* Study empirical robustness, monitoring, Trojan detection, and systemic risk mitigation.
7. **CS 120 / CS 281: Introduction to AI Safety** *(Stanford University)*
   - *Focus:* Explore technical alignment, reward hacking, interpretability, and specification gaming.
8. **CS 224U: Natural Language Understanding** *(Stanford University)*
   - *Focus:* Learn systematic evaluation frameworks, behavioral testing, and bias probing for language models.

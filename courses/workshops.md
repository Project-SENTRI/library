## Recommended Self-Study Path

### Hands-On Red-Teaming & Prompt Injection (Beginner)
1. **HackAPrompt & Jailbreak Labs** *(Learn Prompting)*
   - *Focus:* Watch walkthroughs and practice real-world prompt injection, jailbreaking, and guardrail testing.
2. **OWASP AI Security & Red-Teaming Demos** *(OWASP)*
   - *Focus:* Learn technical penetration testing and vulnerability auditing for LLM applications.

### Safety Concepts & High-Level Alignment (Intermediate)
3. **DeepMind AGI Safety Video Course** *(Google DeepMind)*
   - *Focus:* Understand agent safety, specification problems, and frontier alignment paradigms.
4. **ML4Good Research Video Sprints** *(ML4Good)*
   - *Focus:* Learn technical research methodologies, alignment theory, and safety strategy.

### Deep Technical Engineering & Interpretability (Advanced)
5. **ARENA PyTorch & Transformer Coding Labs** *(Apart Research)*
   - *Focus:* Build transformers from scratch, program mechanistic interpretability tools, and implement RLHF.
6. **Stanford HAI AI Safety & Ethics Workshops** *(Stanford HAI)*
   - *Focus:* Study frontier model evaluations, policy considerations, and catastrophic risk mitigation.

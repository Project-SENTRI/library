## Recommended Self-Study Path

### Foundations & Machine Learning Basics (Beginner)
1. **Practical Deep Learning for Coders** *(Fast.ai)*
   - *Focus:* Build PyTorch intuition and learn practical model training through hands-on video lectures.
2. **Deep Learning Specialization** *(DeepLearning.AI)*
   - *Focus:* Master core deep learning math, CNNs, and Transformer architecture theory.
3. **ChatGPT Prompt Engineering & Security for Developers** *(DeepLearning.AI & OpenAI)*
   - *Focus:* Learn basic prompt structure, system instructions, and defensive guardrails.

### Core Safety, Alignment & Risk (Intermediate)
4. **AI Safety, Ethics, and Society** *(Center for AI Safety)*
   - *Focus:* Watch structured video modules on catastrophic risk, misuse, alignment, and governance.
5. **AI Safety Fundamentals** *(BlueDot Impact)*
   - *Focus:* Dive into technical alignment mechanisms, scalable oversight, and goal misgeneralization.

### Technical RL, Fine-Tuning & Security (Advanced)
6. **Deep Reinforcement Learning Class** *(Hugging Face)*
   - *Focus:* Learn Q-learning and policy gradients essential for understanding RLHF.
7. **Generative AI with LLMs** *(DeepLearning.AI & AWS)*
   - *Focus:* Understand modern fine-tuning, PEFT/LoRA, and RLHF deployment pipelines.
8. **AI Security & Privacy** *(DeepLearning.AI)*
   - *Focus:* Implement technical defenses against adversarial attacks and privacy leakage.

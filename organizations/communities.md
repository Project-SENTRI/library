# AI Safety & Governance Communities

This section highlights global networks, online forums, and field-building communities dedicated to connecting researchers, students, developers, and policy advocates in AI safety and responsible development.

---

## Featured Communities

### 1. AI Alignment Forum (AF)
* **Focus:** Technical Alignment Research, Formal Safety Arguments, Interpretability
* **Description:** A curated discussion platform and research forum where top researchers publish, review, and discuss technical papers and theoretical frameworks related to aligning transformative AI systems.
* **Link:** [Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/)

### 2. LessWrong
* **Focus:** Epistemics, Rationality, Long-Term AI Risk, Safety Discussion
* **Description:** A community blog and discussion platform focused on refining human reasoning and decision-making, serving as one of the foundational hubs for long-form discussion on AI safety and existential risk.
* **Link:** [LessWrong AI Safety](https://www.lesswrong.com/)

### 3. EleutherAI
* **Focus:** Grassroots Open-Source AI, Interpretability, Alignment, Open Models
* **Description:** A non-profit grassroots collective of researchers, engineers, and open-source advocates working on open large-scale AI research, interpretability tools, and safety evaluation.
* **Link:** [EleutherAI Website](https://www.eleuther.ai/) | [Discord Community](https://discord.gg/eleutherai)

### 4. BlueDot Impact
* **Focus:** Talent Acceleration, AI Safety Courses, Global Fellowships
* **Description:** A talent accelerator running global education programs, courses, and communities (such as the AI Safety Fundamentals course) to help students and professionals enter the AI safety ecosystem.
* **Link:** [BlueDot Impact](https://bluedot.org/)

### 5. PauseAI
* **Focus:** Public Advocacy, Policy Awareness, Global AI Governance
* **Description:** A grassroots activist network with global chapters advocating for public awareness, international coordination, and governance frameworks to mitigate risks from superintelligent AI.
* **Link:** [PauseAI Website](https://pauseai.info/)

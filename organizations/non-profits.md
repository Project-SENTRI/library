# Non-Profit Organizations & Think Tanks

This section indexes independent non-profit organizations, policy think tanks, and public-interest groups working to guide AI policy, societal resilience, standards, and safety infrastructure.

---

## Featured Non-Profits

### 1. Center for AI Safety (CAIS)
* **Focus:** Technical Research, Compute Access, Field-Building, Education
* **Description:** A San Francisco-based research and field-building non-profit dedicated to reducing societal-scale risks from AI by providing free compute clusters to safety researchers, publishing educational curricula, and conducting technical research.
* **Link:** [Center for AI Safety](https://safe.ai/)

### 2. Future of Life Institute (FLI)
* **Focus:** Existential Risk Mitigation, AI Governance, Grants, Policy Advocacy
* **Description:** An independent non-profit steering transformative technologies toward benefiting life and mitigating extreme, large-scale risks through open letters, research grants, and international policy engagements.
* **Link:** [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/)

### 3. AI Now Institute
* **Focus:** Algorithmic Accountability, Societal Impact, Policy, Concentration of Power
* **Description:** An independent research institute studying the social and economic implications of AI, focusing on market concentration, public interest technology, surveillance, and algorithmic accountability.
* **Link:** [AI Now Institute](https://ainowinstitute.org/)

### 4. Center for Humane Technology (CHT)
* **Focus:** AI Awareness, Attention Economy, Systemic Risk, Public Policy
* **Description:** A non-profit dedicated to realigning technology with human well-being, raising public and policy awareness about the systemic risks posed by unchecked AI deployment and algorithmic media.
* **Link:** [Center for Humane Technology](https://www.humanetech.com/)

### 5. AI Incident Database (AIID)
* **Focus:** Incident Tracking, Risk Analysis, Preventative Safety
* **Description:** An open-source, crowd-sourced repository that indexes real-world failures, harms, and unexpected behaviors caused by deployed AI systems to help developers and policymakers learn from past mistakes.
* **Link:** [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/)

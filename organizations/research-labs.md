# Technical AI Safety Research Laboratories

This section indexes dedicated technical research labs, independent institutes, and academic centers focused on mechanistic interpretability, evaluations, agent alignment, and formal verification.

---

## Featured Research Labs

### 1. Alignment Research Center (ARC)
* **Focus:** Eliciting Latent Knowledge (ELK), Theoretical Alignment, Model Evaluations
* **Description:** An independent non-profit research organization dedicated to aligning future machine learning models with human interests, with a separate evaluation branch (ARC Evals / METR) focused on frontier model threat assessments.
* **Link:** [Alignment Research Center](https://www.alignment.org/)

### 2. Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)
* **Focus:** Mathematical Alignment Foundations, Decision Theory, Agent Foundations
* **Description:** One of the earliest technical research institutes focused on foundational mathematical and theoretical problems surrounding smarter-than-human artificial intelligence systems.
* **Link:** [MIRI Official Site](https://intelligence.org/)

### 3. METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research)
* **Focus:** Autonomous Capabilities, Red Teaming, Cyber Risk, Frontier Evaluations
* **Description:** Formerly ARC Evals, METR is an independent non-profit research organization that develops methodologies and benchmarks to evaluate dangerous capabilities and autonomy in frontier AI models.
* **Link:** [METR Website](https://metr.org/)

### 4. Redwood Research
* **Focus:** Applied Interpretability, Adversarial Robustness, Control Theory
* **Description:** A technical research organization focusing on applied alignment research, mechanistic interpretability, and practical control techniques to ensure advanced ML systems remain safe under stress.
* **Link:** [Redwood Research](https://www.redwoodresearch.org/)

### 5. Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM)
* **Focus:** Holistic Evaluation (HELM), Open Foundation Models, Transparency
* **Description:** An interdisciplinary research center based at Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI) dedicated to studying, evaluating, and developing foundation models responsibly.
* **Link:** [Stanford CRFM](https://crfm.stanford.edu/)

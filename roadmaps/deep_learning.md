# Phase 2: Neural Networks, Computer Vision & NLP

This roadmap builds advanced neural network expertise, covering core architectures, optimization, computer vision, and NLP aligned with IOAI deep learning requirements.

---

## 1. Neural Network Fundamentals
* **Perceptrons & MLPs:** Multi-Layer Perceptrons, Forward Pass, Activation Functions (ReLU, GELU, Sigmoid, Tanh).
* **Optimization & Training:** Gradient Descent, Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), Backpropagation, Adam/AdamW, Learning Rate Schedules.
* **Regularization & Normalization:** Dropout, Batch Normalization, Layer Normalization, Weight Decay, Early Stopping.

## 2. Computer Vision (CV)
* **Convolutional Networks:** Convolutional Layers, Pooling (Max, Average), ResNet, MobileNet.
* **Tasks & Architectures:**
  * Image Classification.
  * Object Detection (YOLO, SSD).
  * Image Segmentation (U-Net).
* **Generative & Multimodal Vision:**
  * Autoencoders & VAEs.
  * Vision Transformers (ViT).
  * Multimodal Encoders (CLIP).
  * Diffusion Models (Stable Diffusion fundamentals).

## 3. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* **Text Embeddings:** Word2Vec, GloVe, Contextual Embeddings.
* **Sequence Modeling:** RNNs, LSTMs, Encoder-Decoder Architectures.
* **Attention & Transformers:**
  * Self-Attention Mechanism, Scaled Dot-Product Attention, Multi-Head Attention.
  * Transformer Encoder (BERT) & Transformer Decoder (GPT).
* **NLP Applications:** Text Classification, Tokenization, Named Entity Recognition (NER), Machine Translation.

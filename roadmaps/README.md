# AI, ML & Security Learning Roadmaps

This section provides structured, end-to-end learning pathways from foundational math and coding to advanced specializations in machine learning, generative AI, and AI security/safety.

The roadmaps integrate topics from the global **International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI)** syllabus, top university curricula, and industry threat-modeling standards.

---

## Roadmap Index

* **[1. Foundations & Classical ML]:** Mathematics, Python tooling, classical supervised/unsupervised learning, data processing, and search algorithms.
* **[2. Neural Networks & Deep Learning]:** Optimization, modern architectures (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers), vision, and natural language processing.
* **[3. Generative AI, LLMs & Agents]:** Fine-tuning (LoRA), alignment (RLHF), RAG architectures, and agentic workflows.
* **[4. AI Security, Safety & Governance]:** Red-teaming, prompt injection defense, mechanistic interpretability, threat modeling, and AI alignment.

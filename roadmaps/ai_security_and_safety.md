# Phase 4: Specialization in AI Security, Safety & Governance

This roadmap outlines technical specializations in securing ML systems, auditing models for safety risks, and aligning advanced AI models.

---

## 1. AI Security & Red Teaming
* **Adversarial Attacks:** Fast Gradient Sign Method (FGSM), Projected Gradient Descent (PGD), Universal Adversarial Perturbations.
* **LLM Vulnerabilities:** Direct & Indirect Prompt Injections, Jailbreaking, System Prompt Extraction, Data Exfiltration.
* **Supply Chain & Model Security:** Insecure Serialization (Pickle exploits), Data Poisoning attacks, Backdoor Insertion.

## 2. Technical AI Safety & Alignment
* **Scalable Oversight:** RLAIF, Constitutional AI, Automated Critique.
* **Deception & Misalignment:** Reward Hacking, Goal Misgeneralization, Instrumental Convergence, Eliciting Latent Knowledge (ELK).
* **Mechanistic Interpretability:** Transformer Circuits, Induction Heads, Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs), Activation Patching.

## 3. Evaluation & Threat Assessment
* **Benchmark Suites:** WMDP, HarmBench, SWE-bench, Inspect framework.
* **Risk Assessments:** Autonomous Capability Evaluations (METR frameworks), Cyber Threat Modeling, Bio-hazard Risk Audits.

## 4. AI Policy & Governance
* **Compute Governance:** Hardware tracking, Chip controls, Compute thresholds.
* **Auditing Standards:** Third-party auditing, Model Cards, System Cards, Risk Management Frameworks (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act compliance).
